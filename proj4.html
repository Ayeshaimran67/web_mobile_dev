Tanks Battle Maze

Tanks Battle Maze is a two-player 2D game in which two tanks navigate through a maze filled with walls. The players attempt to shoot each other, causing health reduction until one tank is destroyed. The game takes place on a top-down maze battlefield where each player controls a tank. Players navigate through walls and obstacles while attempting to shoot each other, with each successful hit reducing the opponent’s health. The gameplay continues until one tank’s health reaches zero, declaring the other player the winner.
