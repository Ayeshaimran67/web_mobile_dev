Spring Mass System and Second Order ODEs

Spring mass systems are among the most fundamental models in engineering and applied mathematics. They describe how mechanical systems respond to external forces and provide the foundation for analyzing vibrations, oscillations, and dynamic stability in structures, vehicles, aircraft, and control systems. A spring mass system is modeled by a second-order ordinary differential equation because the system's behavior is determined by the relationship between mass, acceleration (the second derivative of displacement), and the forces acting on it, like the spring's restoring force.
