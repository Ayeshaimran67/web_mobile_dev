Cinema Booking System

I built a Cinema Booking System that lets users book movie tickets online, cancel bookings, and view their complete ticket history — all through a smooth, easy-to-use interface designed with Qt Designer, with SQL powering the backend for reliable data storage and management. The system supports two types of users: Admins, who can manage movies, showtimes, and overall system data, and Customers, who can browse available shows, book seats, and track their past bookings. The goal was to create a simple yet functional platform that mimics real-world cinema ticketing, handling everything from user authentication to seat booking and cancellation, all backed by a structured database.
